# Music shortlist from the YouTube Audio Library

Read from the owner's YouTube Studio Audio Library on 2026-10-25. Read-only: nothing was downloaded.

**What the library gave us.** 2,074 tracks were read across eight genres (Cinematic, Ambient, Classical, Jazz & Blues, Dance & Electronic,
Hip-Hop & Rap, Pop, Rock). Every one showed the standard licence text: "You can use this audio track in any of your videos, including
videos that you monetize. No attribution is required." The library labels each track with a genre and one of ten moods (Calm, Inspirational,
Dramatic, Dark, Sad, Bright, Happy, Funky, Angry, Romantic). Those mood labels can be used as they are by the music system.

**What it could not give us.**
- **Popularity.** The library shows no play counts and cannot sort by popularity (only Title, Artist, Duration, Added). A web search found no
  trustworthy ranking either. These picks are by fit, not popularity.
- **Whether a track has vocals.** The listing does not say. Picks lean on genres and producers who make instrumental music. **Listen to about
  30 seconds of each before downloading**, and drop any with singing or rapping if it will sit under speech.

## Notes for the music system (Phase 6)
- All shortlisted tracks are 1:48 to 4:54, so every clip length fits without looping.
- Serious subjects get no music under the existing rule, so the dark and sad group is only for clips that are dramatic, not tragic.
- Record each track's licence text and the date downloaded in the track's record, as the plan says.

## How the app uses this list
The same list, with each track's mood and categories, is in `config/villomillo/music_shortlist.yaml`. Download a track from YouTube Studio's
Audio Library into your Downloads folder, then press **Import from Downloads** on the **Music** tab of the Formats page. The app matches the
file by its name, files it, and keeps its licence record. The tab's "Still to download" list shows what is missing.
