Caption styles

Twelve looks for the words as they are said. These drawings are close, not exact: use Show preview in the builder to see the real thing on a real clip.

Your logo

Upload it once. A template can show it in a corner, and a call to action in the last seconds. Both are for pasted-link and Research clips only; campaign clips never show them.

PNG, WebP or GIF, 2 MB at most, 4000 pixels at most on the longest side. An animated GIF shows its first frame.

Your fonts

Upload a TTF or OTF font for captions and headlines. Each needs where it came from and its licence.

Music library

Songs you download from the YouTube Audio Library. A template can put one quietly under the voice, only where the AI says it helps. With no tracks here, no clip gets music and nothing breaks.

Download a track in YouTube Studio, then press Import. The app finds each shortlisted file by its name, files it with its mood and categories, and keeps its licence record.
