Estimated CPM = ${{ '%.2f'|format(d.cpm_base) }} x category factor x (viral score / {{ d.viral_min }}) until real results exist. Campaigns use their stated pay.

Expected revenue = predicted views ({{ '{:,}'.format(d.predicted_views) }}, a rough guess) x CPM / 1,000. When clips are waiting, the highest goes first.
