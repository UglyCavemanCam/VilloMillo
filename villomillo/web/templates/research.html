Where it looks

YouTube
Creative Commons videos only, from the channels it watches and from searches. A credit line goes in the caption: "Original: [title] by [creator], Creative Commons (CC BY)."
Twitch
Finds the most watched live channels in each topic's category{% if d.has_twitch %}, adds them to the watch list and clips their finished broadcasts{% else %} once your Twitch key is stored: python -m villomillo key twitch{% endif %}. Channels you list below are always used.

Searches Kick for channels in each topic (Kick has no official search, so this can stop working) and clips their finished broadcasts. Channels you list are always used.
